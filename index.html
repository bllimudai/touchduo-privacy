TouchDuo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Package: com.aistudio.touchduo.xkrmpz

TouchDuo ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private two-partner connection app designed for couples. We respect your privacy and collect only the data strictly required to provide real-time syncing between paired partners.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Info: When you sign in with Google, we store your Firebase User ID, display name, optional profile photo URL, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token to authenticate your account, pair you with one partner, and deliver notifications.
  • Couple Content: Heartbeat touches, Magic Canvas strokes, Walkie-Talkie voice snippets, couple chat messages, shared photos, synced wallpapers, mood statuses, and special date milestones are stored in a private Firebase Firestore room accessible only to you and your paired partner.
  • Location Data (Optional): When you grant foreground location permission (and optionally enable the Always-Connected Live Link foreground service), TouchDuo shares your GPS coordinates and resolved city name exclusively with your paired partner. You can pause location sharing anytime inside the app.
  • Battery Status: Battery percentage and charging state are synced with your paired partner to display live status and send 15% low-battery alerts.

2. Permissions We Use

  • Location (Foreground / Optional Live Link): Displays live partner map, distance, and 500m proximity alerts. TouchDuo never tracks location silently in the background without an active user-started notification.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only while you press and hold the Push-to-Talk Walkie-Talkie button.
  • Photos: Selected strictly via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker; TouchDuo never requests broad storage read permissions.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you of Love Bubbles, Chat messages, Walkie clips, Low-Battery alerts, and nearby proximity.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and protected by strict per-room Firebase Firestore Security Rules.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can unpair from your partner or permanently delete your account and couple data at any time directly inside the app via Menu (⋮) → Delete Account & Data. You may also request deletion by emailing us.
